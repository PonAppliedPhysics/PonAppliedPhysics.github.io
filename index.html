Weather Forecasting with ML

End-to-end ML pipeline on 50+ years of Columbus NOAA data. Engineered 12 temporal features and compared Ridge Regression vs. Random Forest — achieving R² = 0.82 with the top feature (TMAX_roll7) carrying 66.9% of importance.

Python scikit-learn NOAA API Feature Engineering Random Forest

Experimental Physics · Physics 5700

Saturated Absorption Spectroscopy of Rubidium

Resolved hyperfine structure of ⁸⁵Rb and ⁸⁷Rb D2 lines at 780.2 nm using sub-Doppler spectroscopy. Achieved ~10 MHz resolution against a 500 MHz Doppler background; ground-state splittings within 3% of accepted values.

Optics Laser Physics Python Analysis Interferometry

Computational Physics · Physics 5700

Thermal Conductivity Modeling — PINNs

Modeled 1D heat diffusion in a copper rod across 5 progressive models: analytic Fourier series → finite-difference (FTCS) → SciPy-optimized → Physics-Informed Neural Network. PINN recovered thermal diffusivity as a learned parameter (MAE = 0.42°C).

TensorFlow PINNs NumPy SciPy Chi-Squared

ABOUT

I'm a Physics BS student at The Ohio State University in the College of Engineering, with coursework spanning experimental physics, optics, quantum mechanics, and mechanical engineering.

My work lives at the boundary between physical theory and practical engineering — whether that's building sensor validation pipelines for Honda's factory floor, machining AUV parts in the campus machine shop, or implementing neural networks that satisfy PDEs as physics constraints.

I'm particularly interested in mechanical engineering, robotics, data science, and ML/AI applied to real physical systems — problems where getting the physics right actually matters.
